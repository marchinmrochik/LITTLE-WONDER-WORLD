# Shape Sorting - desktop 0.6.0

Delivered 2026-11-13. Four of ten educational games now have complete desktop increments. Physical Android acceptance remains pending.

## Delivered behavior

Shape garden / Сад фігур includes Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle, Star and Heart. Original colored pieces match neutral silhouette slots by shape ID. Piece and slot order are independently shuffled; index position cannot decide correctness. Easy/Normal/Advanced have 2/3/4 pieces per board, with three boards per session and all six forms covered in each session. Every piece must be placed before a board advances.

Tap a shape or outline to hear its English name; the footer repeats the selected name. Instruction/reaction helpers follow EN/UK navigation. A drag preview highlights possible targets. A correct drop snaps to the target and leaves the piece there; an incorrect piece animates back. Reduced motion settles immediately. A 12-logical-pixel tolerance is resolved by nearest target center, with filled targets excluded. Outside, occupied or canceled drops do not count as attempts. Unrelated fingers and duplicate releases cannot place a piece. Focus loss, parent overlay, Escape and exit cancel active drags; feedback and snap animations pause when appropriate.

Real attempts, completed placements and sessions save under shapes. Completion/replay works across three boards; progress remains distinct from the previous games. All approved base art/music and the softer Cori/Lada speech processing are retained. Subjective voice/pronunciation approval is still pending.

## Implementation

ShapeBoard is a coordinate-independent layout/matching model. PointerDrag owns one mouse/touch pointer and emits move/release/cancel. DragCard is shared by Shapes and the backwards-compatible FoodCard wrapper; Feeding regression passed. The view owns hit testing, snapping, rendering and feedback. Six source SVGs and six neutral silhouettes are authored locally by prepare_shapes_content.py. shapes.json and localized keys/voice entries are separate from logic. The five-card activity menu fits reviewed ratios with wrapped titles.

22 additional offline WAVs were generated by prefix using the existing licensed models, preserving all previous 110 clips. Current pack: 132 clips, 6,731,468 bytes, duration 0.66-2.74s. No new download, dependency, account or tablet setup required.
